# DEMO_SCRIPT.md

Walk an industry leader through the ZBIF marketplace in about 10 minutes.

1. **Public home** (`/`) — countdown, how it works, featured challenges, sponsors.
2. **Sign in** as `corporate@zbif.test` / `Password123!`.
3. **Submit a challenge** — `/app/challenges/create` → lands in `submitted`.
4. **Sign out**, sign in as `committee@zbif.test` → `/admin/screening` → prioritise → publish.
5. Confirm challenge on public `/challenges`.
6. Sign in as `innovator@zbif.test` → open challenge → **Claim** → development workspace.
7. Update milestones, post feedback, book coaching, **Mark solution ready**.
8. On challenge page, **Recompute matches** (shows why matched).
9. **Express interest** from corporate (or accept a seed connection) → open **Deal Room**.
10. Chat (polls every 3s), move stage to MOU/pilot, tag outcome with public announce.
11. Visit `/deals` to see announcement. Investor: `investor@zbif.test` → `/app/investor`.
12. Admin `organizer@zbif.test` → `/admin/impact` → export Impact Report CSV.
13. Optional: `/register/chat` with Nova, or complete a seeded survey under `/app/surveys`.

Success metrics on the Impact dashboard mirror the concept note: challenges, innovators, universities, solutions, partnerships, adoptions, pilots, MOUs, investment, media, attendance.
